# The Anti-AI-Slop Code in Brand Naming
*How to Eliminate Generic AI Clichés and Enforce World-Class Aesthetic Taste*

Standard language models suffer from severe bias toward generic, synthetic, and cliché naming patterns. When asked to name a company or product, unguided AI defaults to lazy portmanteaus, suffix stuffing, and faux-latin abstractions.

An elite agency naming engine enforces an ironclad blacklist and a rigorous Category Contrast Matrix.

---

## 1. The Banned Suffixes (Suffix Stuffing)

Never propose names created by simply appending trendy tech suffixes:

| Banned Pattern | Why It Fails | Cliché Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `-ify` | Overused to death since Spotify/Shopify (2008). Signals a cheap clone. | *Taskify, Cloudify, Docify, Teamify, Flowify* |
| `-ly` | Outdated Web 2.0 suffix. Dilutes brand seriousness. | *Quickly, Swiftly, Trackly, Cleanly, Smartly* |
| `-io` | Stolen from the ccTLD. Lazy branding crutch. | *Taskio, Brandio, Cloudio, Datio, Syncio* |
| `-sy / -zy` | Infant-like, juvenile, lacks executive credibility. | *Worksy, Appzy, Taskzy, Docsy* |
| `-ix / -ex / -ox` | Fake pharmaceutical / 1990s IT consulting vibe. | *Syncix, Flowex, Datox, Taskex, Cloudix* |
| `-ia / -ora / -ana` | Empty romanticized Latin suffixes tacked onto English words. | *Taskora, Flowana, Cloudia, Dataria* |

---

## 2. The Banned Tech Prefix Mashups

Never smash two generic tech buzzwords together:

- **Banned Prefixes:** `Smart-`, `Omni-`, `Data-`, `Cloud-`, `Neuro-`, `Synap-`, `Meta-`, `Nexa-`, `Hyper-`, `Intel-`, `Cyber-`, `Auto-`.
- **Banned Mashups:**
  - *CloudFlow, DataPulse, OmniSync, SmartSphere, NeuroCore, SynapseIQ, QuantumPath, NovaLink, NexaFlow, HyperSync, DataWave, OmniBridge.*
- **Why It Fails:**
  These names sound like generated placeholder text from a low-tier crypto whitepaper or a generic corporate template. They mean everything and nothing.

---

## 3. The Banned "Empty Latin" Abstractions

Never propose sterile, generic corporate abstracts that have been registered ten thousand times by trademark holding companies:

- **Banned Words:**
  - *Lumina, Apex, Nexus, Vertex, Momentum, Catalyst, Prism, Synergy, Zenith, Verto, Aether, Elysium, Aegis, Pinnacle, Horizon.*
- **Why It Fails:**
  These names communicate zero product personality. Every industry has 40 bankrupt companies named "Nexus" or "Apex".

---

## 4. The Banned Descriptive Snooze-Fests

Never recommend literal feature descriptions:

- *EasyInvoice, QuickCode, FastForm, SimpleMeeting, SafeFiles, CleanMail.*
- **Why It Fails:**
  These are feature descriptions, not brand names. You cannot trademark them, you cannot build brand equity on them, and customers forget them within 30 seconds.

---

## 5. The Category Contrast Matrix (The Law of Uncontested Space)

Before proposing any name, you must audit the competitor landscape across two key axes:
1. **Semantic Tone:** Clinical / Technical vs. Human / Warm / Conversational
2. **Structural Construction:** Real Word / Metaphor vs. Coined / Neologism

### Example: The CRM Landscape
- **Incumbents:** *Salesforce* (literal/enterprise), *HubSpot* (technical hub), *Pipedrive* (mechanical sales pipe).
- **The Cliché Trap:** Proposing *DealFlow*, *PipelineIQ*, *SalesCloud*.
- **The Agency Contrasts:**
  - *Attio* (clean, Italianate architectural warmth)
  - *Folk* (radically human, anti-enterprise community vibe)
  - *Daylight* (clarity and optimism vs dark enterprise databases)

### Example: The Developer Tool Landscape
- **Incumbents:** *Datadog*, *Dynatrace*, *New Relic*, *Splunk*.
- **The Cliché Trap:** Proposing *LogPulse*, *TraceCore*, *MetricSync*.
- **The Agency Contrasts:**
  - *Linear* (geometric, philosophical, fast)
  - *Honeycomb* (natural architecture, hexagonal density, warmth)
  - *Vercel* (coined, aerodynamic speed)

## 6. The 4 Golden Rules of Taste

1. **Restraint Over Noise:** If a name needs an exclamation mark, a gimmick, or an explanation, it is weak. The best names possess quiet, unmistakable confidence.
2. **Physicality Over Abstraction:** Concrete objects (*Stripe, Apple, Anchor, Pebble, Scale*) anchor in the human memory 5x faster than abstract concepts (*Synergy, Velocity*).
3. **Phonetic Texture:** Pay attention to how the lips and tongue move. If saying the name feels like chewing gravel, reject it.
4. **Cultural Depth:** Great names draw on architecture, mythology, literature, astronomy, geography, and craft—not just the Silicon Valley bubble.

---

## 7. The Banned Domain Cop-Outs (No Modifier Hacks)

Never propose a name and then claim it is "domain verified" because an awkward modifier domain is available:
- **Strictly Banned:** Claiming `get{brand}.com`, `{brand}hq.com`, `{brand}app.com`, `try{brand}.com`, or `use{brand}.com` counts as clearing domain viability.
- **The Golden Rule:** If a candidate name's exact `.com` is taken by an active competitor AND all clean modern extensions (`.ai`, `.io`, `.app`) are taken, **the name is DEAD**. Discard it immediately. 
- Founders want clean brand names with clean, authentic domains (`.com`, `.ai`, `.io`, `.app`). Never compromise with hack suffixes.
